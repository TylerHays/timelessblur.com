What's new

What changed in each version, newest first. Your iPhone or iPad usually updates the app on its own; to check which version you have, open the app and look at the bottom of Settings.

Version 1.2

  • Hear the word. Tap the speaker on a flash card and the word is said out loud. If it gives the answer away, turn off Speaker Button on Cards in Settings.
  • Listen & Pick. A new game: your child hears a word and finds it among four. A wrong tile turns red and the word is said again. Finding it on the first tap counts as right, and anything else as wrong, in the same record the flash cards keep.
  • The practice trail. A path across the top of practice shows how far through the session you are, and how each word went.
  • A more natural voice. Choose the voice that says the words in Settings, which also shows how to download a better one from iOS for free.
  • Balloons and Sparkles. Two new ways to celebrate a good session, alongside confetti and fireworks — or let Surprise Me pick.
  • What's new, in the app. After an update the app shows what changed, once. Settings shows it again.

Version 1.1

  • See which way each word is going. A green arrow beside a word means it has been going better lately, an orange one worse — so a word missed all last week and right every time since no longer looks the same as one that is always shaky.
  • The screen stays on during practice. A child sounding out a word is not touching the screen, and it used to dim mid-word. It now stays on while a card is up, and goes back to normal a few minutes after the last tap.
  • Download several lists at once. Tap Select in Download a List, pick as many as you want, and they arrive together. A list you already have only gains the words it was missing, and keeps every score.
  • Start over. Settings can clear every word's practice record, keeping the lists and the words themselves.
  • No celebration for a session that went badly. Set to celebrate every word they get right, it used to throw fireworks however the session had gone. It now waits for a session that earned it, and the bar for that can be set in Settings.
  • Fixes. A crash that could happen when leaving a screen.

Version 1.0

The first release.

  • Start right away with the Dolch Pre-Primer and Primer lists, built in. One tap adds them — no typing and no internet needed.
  • Make your own lists of the words your child is working on, in the app or on a computer with make your own word list.
  • Practice together, one list or several at once, and see at the end of each session which words went well and which need another night.
  • Download a list from the ones published on this site.
  • Share a list with another parent. Only the words go — never your child's practice history.
  • Celebrate a good session with confetti or fireworks, or turn it off.
  • Your lists follow you to your other devices through your own iCloud. We never see them.
