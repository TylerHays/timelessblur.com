Privacy Policy

The short version: Timelessblur apps don't ask for your name, email, or any account. They don't show ads, don't include third-party analytics or tracking, and don't sell or share personal information with anyone.

Who we are

Timelessblur is the name under which Tyler Hays, an independent developer in Austin, Texas, publishes apps and this website. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Timelessblur.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to apps published under the Timelessblur name on the App Store (including our sight words app) and to this website, timelessblur.com.

Information our apps collect

We don't collect personal information. Specifically, our apps:

  • Don't require an account, sign-in, name, email address, or phone number.
  • Don't use advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers.
  • Don't include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • Don't access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone unless a feature clearly needs it and you grant permission. Anything captured that way stays on your device.

Settings and progress are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple may back up or sync that data under Apple's privacy policy; we can't see it.

Downloaded content

Some apps download content from timelessblur.com, such as word lists. These are ordinary requests for public files. They don't include any information about you, and we don't use them to identify or profile anyone.

Like any website, timelessblur.com is hosted by a provider, Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. We don't use that data to identify people.

Children

Some of our apps are designed for young children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. There are no ads, no in-app chat, and no way to share information publicly from within our apps. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Contacting us by email

If you email us, we'll receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above. If an app starts collecting new kinds of data, this page will say so before that update is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@timelessblur.com.
